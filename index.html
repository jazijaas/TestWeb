Why Al Jaddaf

A well-connected part of Dubai on the creek, known for its dhow yards and quick access to the rest of the city.

Al Jaddaf metro station

Green Line access to Old Dubai, Downtown and beyond.

Dubai Creek

Waterfront walks and traditional dhows on your doorstep.

Dubai Healthcare City

Close by for medical visitors and their families.

Old Dubai and Downtown

Souks, museums and the city's main attractions within easy reach.

Dubai International Airport

Roughly a 10 to 15 minute drive, depending on traffic.

Business and leisure

Handy for conferences, appointments and sightseeing alike.

Questions

Quick answers before you book.

How do I book a room?

Email info@jaddafhotelllc.ae or call reception with your dates and the number of guests. We reply with rates and availability.

Can you arrange airport transfers?

Yes, ask us when you book and we will arrange a pickup or drop-off for you.

Is there parking?

Yes, parking is available for guests arriving by car. Let us know if you need a space reserved.

Do you host meetings or events?

We can host business meetings and small gatherings. Contact us with your date and group size for details.

What is nearby?

Al Jaddaf metro station, Dubai Creek, Dubai Healthcare City and the routes to Old Dubai and Downtown are all close by.
